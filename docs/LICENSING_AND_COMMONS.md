# Aura Commons - licensing and ownership boundary

## 1. Paper XI and publication material

CC BY 4.0 governs copyright permissions in the publication. It is not itself a patent license and is not represented here as one.

## 2. Aura World Seed / Gem Arena reference software

The reference software in this repository is licensed under the **Apache License 2.0**.

Apache-2.0 permits use, modification, distribution, and commercial use subject to its terms. Section 3 provides an express contributor patent license for patent claims within the scope defined by that license and contains a patent-litigation termination provision.

## 3. Defensive-publication intent

The Aura architecture, methods, mathematics, combinations, variants, falsifiers, and implementation approaches intentionally disclosed by Paper XI are being placed into the public technical record as defensive prior art.

The project does **not** claim that a Creative Commons license by itself prevents all later patents. The defensive mechanism is the dated, publicly accessible, sufficiently detailed disclosure together with reproducible source/provenance records.

No exclusive patent rights are asserted by this project over the subject matter intentionally contributed to the commons through this disclosure, to the extent the project has the legal ability to make that commitment.

## 4. Developer-created work

Aura does not claim ownership of independently authored downstream works merely because Aura, an Aura agent, the Gem/Arena, a World Seed, the Atomizer, or another Aura tool was used to create, transform, test, inspect, or reason about them.

Rights, if any, remain with the person or entity otherwise entitled to them under applicable law and their agreements.

Using Aura as a tool does not, by project policy, transfer ownership of a developer's independent work to Aura or Dallas Courchene.

## 5. Covered Aura code versus downstream code

Copies and modifications of Apache-2.0-covered Aura code remain subject to the Apache-2.0 notice, attribution, and other conditions.

A downstream work can have its own licensing terms to the extent Apache-2.0 permits and applicable law allows.

## 6. Third-party material

Third-party code, datasets, models, media, and other works retain their own licenses and rights. Nothing in this policy relicenses third-party material.
